# Major Finance - Recent Updates Summary

## 🎉 What's New

We've completed a comprehensive review and enhancement of the Major Finance app, implementing improvements across all teams' recommendations.

---

## ✨ New Features

### 1. Interactive Tutorial Walkthrough
**NEW** - First-time users now get a guided 5-step tour

- **Auto-appears** for new users (0 missions, <200 XP)
- **Step-by-step introduction** to missions, calculators, and AI features
- **Can be skipped** and revisited later from Dashboard
- **Persistent storage** - won't show again once completed
- **Beautiful animations** with progress indicators

**Location:** Appears automatically on Dashboard for new users

---

### 2. Calculator Hub
**ENHANCED** - Financial calculators now prominently featured

#### Compact View (Default)
- 3 calculator cards on Dashboard
- **Retirement Calculator** - Featured with social proof
- **Emergency Fund Calculator** - Quick access
- **TSP Optimizer** - Unlocks after 2 missions

#### Expanded View
- Full calculator hub with 4 tools
- Detailed descriptions and user statistics
- Ratings and estimated completion times
- AI assistance CTA

**Location:** Dashboard → "Financial Calculators" section

---

### 3. Quick Start Guide
**NEW** - Personalized onboarding for new users

- **Recommended missions** based on your progress
- **Visual completion indicators** - see what you've done
- **Time estimates** and XP rewards shown
- **Smart recommendations** - adapts to your level
- **Auto-hides** once you've completed 3 missions

**Location:** Top of Dashboard (for users with <3 completed missions)

---

### 4. Marketing & Feature Announcements
**NEW** - Strategic banners for engagement

#### Three Banner Types:
1. **Premium Upgrade** - For users with 3+ missions
   - Shows Pro features and benefits
   - Limited time offers
   - Clear pricing

2. **Tutorial CTA** - For users who skipped tutorial
   - Re-engagement opportunity
   - Always accessible from bottom of Dashboard

3. **Feature Announcements** - New capabilities
   - AI-powered features
   - New calculators
   - Product updates

**Dismissal:** Banners can be dismissed and won't show again for 24 hours

## 📱 How to Use New Features

### Starting the Tutorial
**Option 1:** Automatic (new users)
- Tutorial appears automatically on first Dashboard visit

**Option 2:** Manual (returning users)
- Scroll to bottom of Dashboard
- Look for "New to Major Finance?" card
- Click "Start Tutorial" button

### Using Calculator Hub
**Quick Access:**
- Dashboard → "Financial Calculators" section
- Click any calculator card to start

**Full Hub View:**
- Dashboard → "Financial Calculators"
- Click "View All" button
- See all 4 calculators with details

### Following Quick Start
**Automatic:**
- Appears at top of Dashboard for users with <3 missions
- Shows 3 recommended missions
- Click any mission to start
- Tracks completion automatically
- Hides after 3 missions completed

### Dismissing Marketing Banners
- Look for X button in top-right corner
- Click to dismiss
- Banner won't show for 24 hours
- Different banner variants rotate
